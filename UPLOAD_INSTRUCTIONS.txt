IMPORTANT V2 BEHAVIOUR
1. V2 automatically creates a new paper account named primary-v2, starting from configured paper_starting_cash (normally $10,000).
2. Old V1 accounts/trades remain in the database but are excluded from V2 P/L and live-gate calculations.
3. V2 signals are version-tagged, so V1 signals cannot trigger V2 trades.
4. The same completed candle cannot be traded twice.
5. A 60-minute re-entry cooldown applies after a V2 trade closes.
6. A setup must CLOSE above the previous 20-candle high to count as a breakout.
7. Breakouts require adequate volume, acceptable RSI, supportive trend, supportive BTC regime and acceptable spread.
8. Extended breakouts are rejected instead of chased.
9. Volume scoring is smoothed instead of frequently collapsing to 0/100.
10. Paper entries use current Binance price and reject signals after excessive price drift.
11. Paper stop/target detection uses 1-second Binance candles between worker checks.
12. If stop and target are both touched in the same 1-second candle, the paper engine assumes stop first (conservative).
13. Actual paper/live targets are recalculated from the achieved entry so the configured R multiple is preserved.
14. Total exposure is checked AFTER including the proposed new position.
15. Backtests use historical BTC regime data, matching live scanning logic.
16. Backtest entry fees are no longer double-counted.
17. Backtests use mark-to-market equity for more realistic drawdown measurement.
18. Scanner defaults to a curated liquid/core universe instead of admitting meme/newly speculative pairs solely because of volume.
19. Live reconciliation still protects any already-open exchange positions, including older-version positions.
20. Live trading remains governed by the paper live gate and environment safety switches.
