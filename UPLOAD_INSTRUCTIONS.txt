WHAT IT ADDS
- Candidate Validation Lab v2.3 for the four research candidates:
  * INJUSDT 1h
  * ATOMUSDT 1h
  * ATOMUSDT 4h
  * TRXUSDT 4h
- Threshold grid: 72, 75, 78, 80, 82, 85.
- Threshold 80 is explicitly marked as the forward-paper hypothesis, not retrospectively selected by the grid.
- Each threshold is fully replayed; lower-score trades are not merely filtered after the fact.
- Separate Candidate paper account starting from the configured paper starting cash (normally $10,000).
- Candidate paper universe: INJUSDT + ATOMUSDT, 1h, score >= 80.
- Candidate paper trades use the same fees, slippage, stop/target and risk sizing as the control, plus a 24-hour maximum hold to mirror the research engine.
- Candidate trades are tagged strategy_version="2.3-candidate", so they DO NOT enter the Strategy v2.0 live-gate statistics.
- The normal Strategy v2.0 paper account remains unchanged as the control.
- market_worker runs both the normal control cycle and the candidate cycle every worker interval.

AFTER DEPLOYMENT
1. Open Backtests.
2. Scroll to “Candidate Validation v2.3”.
3. Click “Run all candidate validations (4)”.
4. Keep the tab open until all four are stored.
5. Open Paper Trading.
6. You will see the original Strategy v2.0 control account and a separate Candidate v2.3 account.
7. The Render market worker will automatically evaluate both experiments after the new market_worker.py is deployed/restarted.

IMPORTANT
- This does NOT enable live trading.
- This does NOT loosen the existing live gate.
- Candidate results are an experiment. The 2026 data has already informed the hypothesis, so future unseen paper trades are the stronger validation.
