What this adds
--------------
Closed Paper Trading Lab rows now show:
Pair | Entry time | Score | BTC regime | Entry | Stop | Target | Exit | Reason | Hold | P/L | R

Open positions also show the entry decision context and holding time.

No database migration is required.

Important behavior
------------------
- New trades freeze the BTC regime and the complete signal-factor snapshot in Trade.metadata at entry.
- Existing Strategy v2 trades (including the already-closed INJUSDT trade) attempt to recover BTC regime from their original MarketSignal using metadata.signal_id. If that original signal no longer exists, BTC regime displays as an em dash rather than inventing a value.
- R is calculated as net P/L divided by the initial risk_amount stored on the trade.
- Hold is calculated from opened_at to closed_at, or from opened_at to the current time for an open position.
