RESEARCH CANDIDATE CRITERIA
---------------------------
A result is only flagged as a research candidate when ALL are true:
- at least 20 out-of-sample trades;
- out-of-sample net return > 0;
- out-of-sample profit factor >= 1.25;
- out-of-sample expectancy R > 0;
- at least two evaluable 90-day windows;
- at least 60% of evaluable windows are positive.

This is a research label only. It is not a live-trading gate and does not imply future profitability.
